# SAME / SAME-R

- In microgravity, smoke transport was dominated by mixing, and detection lags were longer than in normal gravity. [SAME final report, NASA TM / NIST, https://www.nist.gov/node/1863886]
- SAME-R sample airflow ranged from quiescent to 8 cm/s, smoke was aged from 11 to 1800 s, and a prototype two-moment detector was flown. [NTRS 20130000422]
- Most SAME-R average smoke particle sizes were 200 to 400 nm; quiescent cases were sometimes larger. [NTRS 20130000422]
- The SAME moment method uses the 0th (number), 1st (diameter) and 3rd (mass) moments, with a lognormal assumption to derive geometric mean diameter and spread. [Yang et al., Aerosol Sci. Technol. 49 (2015)]
